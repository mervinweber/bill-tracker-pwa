# Bill Tracker PWA Todo

## Top priorities
1. Global search across bills, notes, categories, and payee details.
2. Calendar-first planning view for due dates and pay periods.
3. Rich bill detail drawer with history, payment actions, and bill metadata.
4. Cash-flow forecasting with paycheck-aware projections.
5. Better reminder controls, including snooze presets and per-bill reminder tuning.

## Strong follow-ups
6. Search and filter shortcuts that work well on mobile and narrow desktop widths.
7. CSV export alongside JSON backup/export.
8. Stronger empty states for setup, no-results, and no-pay-period views.
9. Smaller overflow actions in the bill grid and detail surfaces.
10. More bill metadata fields such as pay URL, account name, autopay, and notes.

## Notes
- The app already has recurring bills, reminders, payment history, categories, pay-period planning, debt tracking, import/export, household sharing, and PWA support.
- The current implementation already improved the header, zero states, bill grid density, and settings flow.
- The next work should focus on features that improve everyday navigation and the "find a bill fast" experience.
